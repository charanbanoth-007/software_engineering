Outpatient Follow-up Coordination - Example Source

The service shall create a follow-up task when a care-team member records that a patient needs additional contact after an outpatient visit.
Each task shall show its current status, assigned care-team role, due date, and next action.
Only authorized care-team members shall be able to view or update tasks within their assigned service area.
The system shall record task creation, assignment changes, status changes, and completion in an auditable event history.
If a task is not completed by its due date, the system shall notify the assigned role and make the overdue task visible to the designated team lead.
When a follow-up relates to a result that requires clinical review, the task shall be routed to a qualified clinician under the organization's approved clinical workflow.
The system shall not interpret results, recommend treatment, or close a clinically flagged task without an authorized care-team decision.
If a notification or connected system is unavailable, the task shall remain visible as pending and the failure shall be recorded for authorized staff to resolve.
Patient-facing messages shall use organization-approved content and contact preferences, and shall not disclose sensitive details in an unapproved channel.
The organization shall define access, retention, escalation, and response-time policies before the service is used in care delivery.
